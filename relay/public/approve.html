LootDrop

Approve your NFTs for in-world item drops

Connect your wallet on Polygon to approve NFTs for LootDrop.

Option 1: Approve entire collection (recommended)

Approves the escrow contract for all items in this collection — current and future. One transaction, drop anything from this collection.

Option 2: Approve a single item

Approves one specific token. You'll need to do this for each item you want to drop.
